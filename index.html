The link phase Uᵢ = exp(−igAᵢ Δx) makes the discrete scalar gradient gauge-covariant. The Gauss-law residual monitors ∇·E − 2g Im(Φ* Φ̇). The magnetic energy is computed from plaquettes. This is a reduced Abelian model; the Standard Model Higgs is an SU(2) doublet, not this single U(1)-charged complex scalar.

The quench starts near an unstable symmetric-field configuration; vortex pair seeds opposite phase windings; pulse creates a radial Higgs displacement. These are initial conditions, not measurements or predictions.

Coverage of the supplied Standard Model action

  • Gauge kinetic terms: one dynamic classical Abelian analogue; SU(3) gluons and SU(2) weak bosons are not evolved.
  • Higgs kinetic and quartic potential: dynamically evolved in reduced U(1) field form.
  • Fermion Dirac fields: omitted; no spinors, quantum fluctuations or particle production.
  • Yukawa interactions: illustrative mass relation only; no coupling back into the lattice.
  • QCD θ₃ term: omitted; no non-Abelian topological sectors.
  • Quantum effects: no path integral, renormalization, vacuum loops or stochastic quantum sampling.

Interpretation: A numerical exploration of symmetry breaking, vortices and gauge dynamics. It cannot establish new physics, calculate Standard Model scattering amplitudes or test experimental claims.
